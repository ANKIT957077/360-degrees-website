THE PROBLEM

Context & Strategic Challenge

The client was preparing to introduce an enterprise-grade collegiate product into the Indian market. Despite strong early product indicators, leadership lacked empirical clarity on unit economic viability, channel willingness to pay, and regional demand variances across Tier-1 and Tier-2 higher education institutions.

Without granular ground-truth research, committing substantial go-to-market capital carried significant commercial downside. The mandate required rapid validation of addressable market size, competitor moat vulnerabilities, and customer friction points.

OUR APPROACH

Field Research & Analytical Frameworks

360 assembled a dedicated four-member advisory pod combining strategic synthesis from IIT Delhi with financial and consumer modeling from SRCC. The engagement deployed a mixed-methods research framework across primary field data and secondary industry benchmarking.

Over six weeks, the pod executed:

  • Primary Survey Corpus: Over 1,200 structured survey responses across 14 university campuses nationwide.
  • In-Depth Expert Interviews: 45 structured qualitative interviews with university department heads, placement officers, and procurement administrators.
  • Competitive Teardown: Feature-by-feature benchmarking of five incumbent offerings against pricing and adoption hurdles.
WHAT WE DELIVERED

Outputs, Models & Actionable Playbooks

  • Bottom-Up TAM/SAM Market Sizing Model: An interactive spreadsheet model evaluating commercial opportunity across tier-based institutional archetypes.
  • Pricing Elasticity & Packaging Architecture: Recommended tiering tiers and contract structuring to maximize annual contract value (ACV).
  • Campus Distribution Playbook: Step-by-step operational guide for collegiate ambassador programs and institutional partnerships.
  • Executive Decision Pack: Synthesized board presentation outlining go-to-market phase gates, capital allocation priorities, and risk mitigations.
IMPACT & OUTCOME

Measurable Change & Client Verdict

Armed with empirical field data, the client's executive leadership avoided an estimated $350K in exploratory go-to-market expenditure on unviable product features. The board unanimously ratified the pod's recommended 3-phase rollout roadmap, targeting high-conversion institutional clusters first.

“The 360 team brought exceptional rigour and intellectual speed to our strategic dilemma. Their primary ground data gave our leadership complete conviction to proceed with clarity.” — Founder & CEO, Confidential Client Organization
